🎓 TOEFL Success Story: 110/120 Achieved!

I'm thrilled to share that I've achieved a score of 110 on the TOEFL iBT exam! This milestone brings me one step closer to my dream of studying at top universities in the US and UK.

📊 Score Breakdown:
• Reading: 28/30
• Listening: 29/30
• Speaking: 26/30
• Writing: 27/30

🎯 This score exceeds the requirements for:
• Harvard University: 105+
• MIT: 100+
• Stanford University: 100+
• Yale University: 100+
• Princeton University: 108+

💡 Key Strategies That Helped:
• Practiced with official ETS materials daily for 3 months
• Joined English conversation groups to improve speaking fluency
• Watched TED Talks and academic lectures to enhance listening skills
• Read academic articles from The Economist and Scientific American
• Took weekly practice tests to track progress and identify weak areas
• Worked with Groupe BM tutors for personalized feedback and strategies

🙏 Grateful for the guidance and support from Groupe BM, who provided excellent resources and mentorship throughout my preparation journey.
